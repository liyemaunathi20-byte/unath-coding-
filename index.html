Our Mission


SchoolHop Foundation is a public transport initiative created to assist learners living in rural areas who travel long distances to school every day.


Many primary and high school learners walk several kilometres daily to reach their schools. The SchoolHop Foundation aims to provide safe, reliable and free transportation funded through public and government partnerships.


  • Improve school attendance
  • Improve learner safety
  • Reduce travel fatigue
  • Increase access to education
  • Support community development
